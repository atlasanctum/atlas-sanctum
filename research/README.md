# research/

Open research produced by and for the Atlas Sanctum platform.

All research is published under **CC BY 4.0** unless otherwise noted.
Indigenous knowledge contributions are governed by **FPIC** and community data sovereignty protocols.

## Structure

```
research/
├── climate/                — Climate modeling, carbon verification methodologies, tipping points
├── governance/             — Constitutional AI, DAO design, institutional resilience
└── indigenous-knowledge/   — Ecological knowledge systems (community-governed access)
```

## Active Research Areas

### Climate
- Carbon sequestration verification methodologies
- Biodiversity co-benefit quantification
- Climate tipping point early warning systems
- Regenerative agriculture carbon accounting

### Governance
- Constitutional AI alignment frameworks
- Bioregional governance models
- Seven-generation planning methodologies
- DAO participation and legitimacy

### Indigenous Knowledge
- Biocultural mapping protocols
- Traditional ecological knowledge integration
- Data sovereignty frameworks (CARE Principles)
- Community-led verification systems
